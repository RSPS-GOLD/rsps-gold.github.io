UNDERSTANDING SPAWNPK GOLD

How SpawnPK Gold and Cash Bags Work

Cash Bags are not a separate SpawnPK currency. Each Cash Bag represents 100M coins and is used to store or transfer larger gold values beyond the normal coin-stack limit.

SAME GOLD VALUE

Cash Bags Represent Gold Coins

One Cash Bag holds the same value as 100M SpawnPK coins. Converting coins into bags does not create a different currency or change the underlying value.

1T Means One Trillion in Gold

An order written as 10T means ten trillion in total SpawnPK gold value. The final transfer may use Cash Bags, coins or an agreed combination.

Why Cash Bags Are Used

A normal coin stack is limited to roughly 2.147B coins. Cash Bags make it practical to hold and transfer the much larger values used in SpawnPK’s economy.

PLAYER-SUPPLIED SPAWNPK GOLD

How Does SpawnPK Gold Reach Our Stock?

The SpawnPK gold we sell comes from other players—people just like you who have built up more in-game wealth than they currently need.

Some sellers get lucky while PvMing and receive a valuable drop. Others build their bank through PvP, merching or a lucky win at ::gamble. When they have more gold than they need, they may decide to sell some of it.

After an accepted sell order is completed, the gold can later be offered to another player. Because our stock depends on players choosing to sell, the amount available and the current rate can change throughout the market.

Why Trill Rates Move

Available stock changes as players sell gold and buyers place orders. Larger orders, fewer sell offers or increased demand may affect the rate currently available.
