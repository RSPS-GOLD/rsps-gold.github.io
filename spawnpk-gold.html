UNDERSTANDING SPAWNPK GOLD

How SpawnPK Gold and Cash Bags Work

Each Cash Bag represents 100M coins. Turning coins into Cash Bags does not change their value; it simply makes larger amounts easier to store and trade.

SAME GOLD VALUE

Cash Bags Represent Gold Coins

Cash Bags hold the same gold value as the coins they represent.

1T Means One Trillion in Gold

An order for 10T means ten trillion in total SpawnPK gold. The transfer can be made with Cash Bags, coins or a mix of both.

Why Cash Bags Are Used

A normal coin stack is limited to roughly 2.147B coins, so Cash Bags are useful for the much larger amounts traded on SpawnPK.

PLAYER-SUPPLIED SPAWNPK GOLD

How Does SpawnPK Gold Reach Our Stock?

The SpawnPK gold we sell comes from other players, just like you. Some get lucky while PvMing and receive a valuable drop. Others build their bank through PvP, merching or a lucky win at ::gamble. When they have more gold than they need, they may decide to sell some of it.

Once a sell order is completed, the gold can be offered to another buyer. Since stock comes from players, both the amount available and the rate can change over time.

Why Trill Rates Move

Larger orders, fewer sell offers or higher demand can affect the rate.
