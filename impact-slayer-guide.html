IMPACT COMBAT PROGRESSION

Impact Slayer Guide

Slayer gives an Impact account a repeatable reason to fight different monsters, improve equipment and learn new locations. Good progression comes from completing suitable tasks consistently, not forcing every assignment with the most expensive setup available.

FIRST ASSIGNMENT

Getting Started With Impact Slayer

The official Getting Started guide says Duradel is in the right-side building at ::home. He assigns tasks, can cancel, block or unblock assignments and provides access to the Slayer store. Impact’s command list also identifies ::slayer as a teleport to your Slayer Master, which can shorten the return trip when the command is available.

Before accepting a task, prepare a basic combat setup for the style your account uses reliably. You do not need a perfect loadout for the first assignment. You need a weapon that works for the target, defensive equipment appropriate to the location and enough supplies to learn how long a trip lasts.

Read the task information before teleporting. The official guide says an Enchanted Gem from Duradel’s store can communicate with him remotely, show the current task location and display the remaining kills. That makes it a useful planning tool once obtained. The Slayer Log in the Accounts tab can also help you review kills and streaks.

COMPLETE, CANCEL OR BLOCK

Make Deliberate Task Decisions

A task can be good for experience, useful drops, variety or simply maintaining a streak. Those goals are not always the same. Before spending points or time, ask whether the assignment fits your equipment, location knowledge and current account target.

Cancellation solves a task you do not want to finish now. Blocking is a longer-term decision for assignments that repeatedly conflict with your plans. Do not block a task after one inconvenient trip without understanding the cost and your broader task pool. Keep notes on which tasks are slow because of weak gear and which are genuinely poor fits; an equipment upgrade may change the first category.

Complete

Use suitable tasks to build consistency, learn locations and progress the account. Record supply use instead of guessing.

Cancel

Consider a one-time cancellation when an assignment clashes with the current session or your available setup.

Block

Reserve blocks for repeated, well-understood problems. Review the current in-game costs before committing points.

SUSTAIN THE TASK

Gear, Supplies and Efficiency

Build a Slayer loadout around repeatability. Start with the required combat style and any target-specific protection or item confirmed by current in-game information. Then add damage, accuracy, prayer or defensive bonuses according to what actually limits the trip. A costly upgrade that saves little time may be less useful than a supply reserve or a second setup for another style.

Bank organization matters because Slayer changes targets. Impact’s Quick Gear system can save and equip different setups, and the official guide describes it as useful for moving between PvP, PvM and skilling loadouts. Even without many saved slots, keep Slayer weapons, common armour and consumables in clear bank tabs so the next task starts quickly.

For longer sessions, track food, prayer restoration, ammunition, runes and degradable charges. Leave enough inventory space for expected loot, but do not assume a specific drop. When a location is dangerous or in the Wilderness, reassess risk separately and carry only what you are willing to lose.

SPEND WITH A PURPOSE

Slayer Rewards and Account Progression

Duradel provides the Slayer store, but store contents and costs can change. Inspect the current store before deciding how to spend points. A useful purchase is one that improves many future tasks or supports a stated account goal; saving points can be better than buying something only because it is available.

Slayer also connects to broader progression. Tasks expose gaps in combat styles, supply planning and location knowledge. A repeated need for ranged damage may justify one upgrade, while short prayer trips may point toward a different improvement. Use completed tasks as evidence for the next purchase.

Impact’s official Money Making page recommends Slayer for newcomers because tasks can provide loot while building combat stats and points. That does not create a fixed profit rate. Task type, account mode, gear, drops and market conditions all affect the outcome.

LONGER-TERM TARGETS

Boss Slayer and Readiness

Some Slayer progression can lead toward boss encounters, but do not jump to a boss only because a task relates to it. Confirm current Slayer requirements, access, mechanics and death risk on the official Wiki. For example, the official Araxxor guide states a Slayer requirement and explains encounter-specific mechanics; that is a separate preparation problem from ordinary task completion.

Use normal assignments to establish reliable prayer switching, supply use and gear changes. When considering a boss, make a separate checklist for entry, required items, attack styles and a learning budget. Boss drops are not guaranteed, and stronger gear does not replace mechanics practice.

PROTECT YOUR PROGRESS

Common Slayer Mistakes

  • Teleporting before checking the task location or remaining kills.
  • Spending points without reviewing current costs and future priorities.
  • Blocking tasks based on one poor trip rather than a repeated pattern.
  • Using valuable gear in a dangerous location without checking risk.
  • Ignoring ammunition, charges or prayer supplies until the trip ends early.
  • Treating a possible boss drop as guaranteed income.

BEFORE EACH TRIP

Impact Slayer Task Checklist

  1. Confirm the assignment, target location and remaining kills.
  2. Check current target requirements and location risk.
  3. Load the correct combat style and essential protection.
  4. Bring sustainable supplies and leave useful inventory space.
  5. Decide whether the goal is completion, learning, points or loot.
  6. After the task, record what equipment or supply limited the trip.
Server-rules note: Follow Impact’s current rules in every task area, including the restrictions on automation and bug exploitation. Check Wilderness risk before carrying valuable equipment.
