QUICK ANSWER

What is the best money-making method in Impact RSPS?

There is no universal best method. New players should generally begin with Slayer, which combines loot with combat progression and Slayer points. A lower-geared player can also join an active boss mass when the group, access and mechanics suit the account.

High-level options named by the official Money Making page include Phantom Muspah, Tombs of Amascut, Theatre of Blood, Chambers of Xeric and the Desert Treasure II bosses. PvP can award coins, Seasonal Event Pearls, Blood Money and Bounty Points, but its result is usually less consistent than repeatable PvM because deaths and replacement costs matter.

Choose by repeatability. Compare access, supply cost, attention, death risk and several completed sessions—not one lucky drop.

BUILD THE FIRST RESERVE

Best beginner money-making methods in Impact RSPS

A first method should be affordable to repeat and useful even when the rare drop does not arrive. These routes prioritize account development, manageable costs and current official guidance.

PRIMARY START01

Slayer with Duradel

Duradel is in the right-side building at ::home, and the current Commands page confirms ::slayer as a direct route to the Slayer Master. Tasks can provide loot, combat progress and points for the Slayer store, which makes Slayer the strongest general beginner recommendation.

The Enchanted Gem can contact Duradel remotely, show the task location and report remaining kills. Use that information to prepare the right style and supplies rather than carrying one expensive setup everywhere.

Read the Impact Slayer Guide
SESSION BOOST02

Voting before PvM

The Getting Started page currently lists 4M GP and one Vote Token per vote, with possible double rewards when a bonus is active. It also lists a 12-hour +5% drop-rate boost and +25% PK drop-value boost.

Type ::vote to open the current interface. Verify the live reward screen before planning around any amount or bonus because voting rewards can change.

REPEATABLE COMBAT03

Armoured Zombies

Use the Spiritual Fairy Tree, choose Dungeons, then Zemouregal's Base. The area is multicombat and contains aggressive packs. The official page currently estimates around 500K coins per zombie and lists the Zombie Axe as a possible tradeable drop.

Blood Barrage is recommended for sustain because some zombies use ranged attacks even while Protect from Melee covers melee damage. Full Ahrim's with an Amulet of the Damned is the wiki's listed budget example; its roughly 19M estimate is historical guidance, not a permanent market price. Check the Trading Post first.

ALTERNATIVES04

Accessible training routes

Scurrius, Catacombs of Kourend, Araxytes and other suitable training zones can be alternatives when Slayer or Armoured Zombies do not fit the account. Their value depends on current drops, kill speed, supplies and what the account still needs to train.

Use ::drops or the in-game Drop Tables interface, then check realistic completed-sale information before choosing. Do not turn a listed rare item into an assumed hourly return.

GROUP OPPORTUNITY

Mass bossing for lower-geared accounts

An active mass can reduce the individual setup burden and let an account participate earlier. It does not remove access checks, encounter mechanics, supply costs or the need to contribute. Activity is not guaranteed, and joining a group does not guarantee a valuable reward.

Nex

Check current access, team activity and a repeatable supply budget before joining.

Scurrius

An accessible group target that can also support combat practice and early progression.

Nightmare

The dedicated guide describes an advanced encounter and recommends a Scythe outside a mass. Group size does not remove prayers, movement or pillar mechanics.

Hueycoatl

The official Money Making route names it as a mass opportunity. Confirm live access, mechanics and drops in game before the session.

ADVANCED PVM

Best high-level money makers in Impact RSPS

The official Money Making page groups these routes among high-level profit options, but it does not make their practical return equal. Gear fit, completion rate, supply use and learning cost determine the useful result.

SOLO SPECIALIST

Phantom Muspah

Muspah is officially listed as a high-level money maker, while its dedicated guide adds an important warning: welfare kills are possible but slow, and a Scythe of Vitur is strongly recommended for efficient attempts.

The dedicated guide also says the drop table is not built around 1B+ jackpot items. Judge repeatable kills, Venator Shards, Ancient Essence and current market demand—not a single imagined payout.

RAID ROUTES

ToA, ToB and CoX

Each raid has different mechanics, team decisions and gear needs. Specialize in one route first and separate learning costs from the return of mature completions. Completion consistency matters more than the theoretical value of a unique.

ToA guide ToB guide CoX guide

BOSS SPECIALIZATION

Desert Treasure II bosses

The official Duke guide calls Duke the easiest DT2 starting point. Vardorvis and Whisperer demand more reactive movement and prayer changes, while Leviathan also rewards precise prayer and movement execution.

Choose the boss matching the account's strongest combat setup. Track deaths, supplies and preparation time, and avoid spreading the bank across four incomplete loadouts.

HIGH VARIANCE

Making GP through PvP in Impact

The official Money Making page lists coins, Seasonal Event Pearls, Blood Money and Bounty Points as possible PvP rewards, while also describing PvP as less consistent than PvM. A win is not net profit until food, potions, ammunition, charges and lost sets have been deducted.

Set a replacement budget

Decide how many complete sets the bank can lose without ending the activity or damaging normal progression.

Track net results

Record banked rewards and every death cost. Unclaimed or unsold rewards are not completed profit.

Use a familiar bracket

Choose a build and combat bracket you understand. Better gear creates options but does not guarantee kills.

Trading is secondary bank management, not automatic profit. A Trading Post listing is not a completed sale. Gambling and Flower Poker are not dependable money-making methods and can lose the stake completely.

How to measure profit fairly

Net profit = loot sold or retained − supplies and charges − repairs, entry fees and losses

  • Compare equal session lengths and record every cost.
  • Use realistic completed-sale prices, not the most optimistic listing.
  • Repeat several sessions before judging consistency.
  • Separate learning attempts from established method performance.

PROTECT THE RESULT

Common Impact money-making mistakes

Chasing the largest possible drop

A rare item is upside, not the baseline for a repeatable method.

Ignoring consumable costs

Runes, food, charges, repairs, entry fees and deaths all reduce the result.

Buying gear before learning

Advanced equipment cannot replace prayers, movement and encounter knowledge.

Risking the entire bank

Keep the session reserve and PvP replacements outside the upgrade budget.

Overvaluing unsold items

A high listing is not the same as a realistic completed sale.

Switching after one bad session

Random results need several comparable sessions before they show a pattern.

Using automation

Low-attention does not mean unattended. Impact's rules prohibit bots, scripts, macros and other automation.

Gambling can lose the entire stake. Do not treat Flower Poker or another chance-based activity as dependable bank progression.

FAQ

Impact money-making questions

What is the best money-making method for a new player in Impact RSPS?

Slayer is the strongest general starting route because tasks combine loot, combat progression and Slayer points. Voting and accessible combat methods can support that progression.

Can low-level players make money through bossing?

Yes. Active Nex, Scurrius, Nightmare or Hueycoatl masses can lower the individual gear burden, but mechanics, access, supplies and group availability still matter.

What are the best high-level GP methods in Impact?

The official Money Making page lists Phantom Muspah, ToA, ToB, CoX and Desert Treasure II bosses among high-level routes. The practical choice depends on gear, mechanics and repeatable completions.

Is Phantom Muspah good money without a Scythe?

The dedicated Muspah guide says welfare kills are possible but slow without a Scythe of Vitur and notes that the boss is not built around 1B-plus jackpot items.

Is PvP better money than PvM?

Not consistently. PvP can award several currencies, but deaths, replacement sets and consumables make its net result more variable than repeatable PvM.

How do I reach Armoured Zombies?

Use the Spiritual Fairy Tree, open the Dungeons category and select Zemouregal's Base. Check current requirements and Drop Tables before the trip.

How should I compare GP per hour?

Compare equal session lengths and deduct supplies, charges, repairs, entry fees and losses. Repeat several sessions and separate learning attempts from established performance.

Should I spend the full bank on an upgrade?

No. Protect a supply and replacement reserve, then buy the smallest upgrade that solves the method's real limiting factor.
