PLAYER BENEFITS

Match benefits to recurring play

Traders and collectors

Compare Trading Post, bank, kit and friends-list capacity. The Royal and Divine jumps are material only when lower limits are genuinely restrictive.

PvM and raids

Extreme starts the instance-and-routing layer. Higher tiers add cumulative drop and CoX bonuses, while Royal and Divine add exclusive content.

Slayer players

Higher tiers increase points and reduce cancellation and block costs. Divine adds the strongest totals and dedicated areas.

PvP players

Legendary is the first command-focused tier. Compare blacklist space, target skips and cooldowns, but never treat rank access as protection from loss.

ROYAL DONATOR

Royal is the first broad endgame utility tier

Royal combines a large capacity jump with Royal Castle, skilling-dungeon and Revenant access, plus AFK Kingdom, custom-kit, spellbook, clue and minimap conveniences. The documented Royal top floor and dungeon carry PvP restrictions, including limited escape rules; check the live warning before entering.

  • 30 Trading Post slots and 1,248 bank slots
  • 40% PvM bonus, 50% CoX bonus and 50% extra Slayer points
  • Royal areas, utilities and ::ptp documentation

DIVINE DONATOR

Divine adds the maximum documented access layer

Divine adds dedicated PvM, Slayer, skilling and AFK spaces, PvM overloads, amethyst, redwoods, Divine implings, PK-key shortcuts, five saved teleports and the periodic Divine Glod encounter. Some content is Wilderness or PvP; exclusivity is not safety.

  • 40 Trading Post slots and 2,048 bank slots
  • 45% PvM bonus, 60% CoX bonus and 75% extra Slayer points
  • Five saved teleports and 60 custom kits

EDITORIAL RECOMMENDATIONS

Choose by the first meaningful unlock

Donator or Super

Choose Donator for basic access. Move to Super when safe-zone banking and the first capacity jump will save time every session.

Extreme or Legendary

Extreme suits repeat PvM routing. Legendary suits active players who will repeatedly use its restoration and unskull commands.

Royal

Royal is strongest when you will combine its zones, capacity and utilities—not when only one percentage attracts you.

Divine

Divine fits players who will use multiple exclusive PvM, Slayer, skilling and convenience loops over time.

These are independent editorial profiles, not official value or savings claims.

FAQ

Roat Pkz Donator-rank questions

How many Roat Pkz Donator ranks are there, and are benefits cumulative?

The official benefits table documents six permanent ranks: Donator, Super, Extreme, Legendary, Royal and Divine Donator. The ladder is cumulative, so each higher rank keeps the documented lower-rank benefits and adds new ones, subject to live changes.

Which ranks first unlock ::bank, ::spec, ::hp and ::unskull?

Super Donator is the first rank documented to unlock ::bank in safe zones. Legendary Donator is the first to unlock ::spec, ::hp and ::unskull.

Which rank first unlocks free Kraken instances and related teleports?

Extreme Donator is the first rank documented with free Kraken instances, plus free Jad and Skotizo teleports.

What does Royal Donator unlock?

Royal Donator adds Royal areas, AFK Kingdom access, custom-kit and spellbook shortcuts, minimum-step clues, minimap utilities and stronger cumulative capacity, PvM, raid and Slayer benefits.

What does Divine Donator unlock?

Divine Donator adds Divine PvM, Slayer, skilling and AFK areas, PvM overloads, high-level gathering, Divine implings, five saved teleports and Divine Glod access.

Which rank provides the strongest PvM, Slayer and Trading Post benefits?

Divine Donator has the highest documented PvM drop-rate bonus at 45%, Slayer-point bonus at 75% and Trading Post capacity at 40 slots. It also has the lowest documented task cancellation and block costs.

Do Roat Pkz Donator drop-rate bonuses guarantee drops?

No. A documented drop-rate bonus changes chance, not certainty. Results still depend on the live drop table, encounter and random outcomes.

Are Royal and Divine areas always safe?

No. Some Royal and Divine content is in PvP, Wilderness or singles-plus areas. Check the live area warning and protect valuable gear before entering.

Are Donator ranks and VIP ranks the same system?

No. The six Donator ranks are the permanent benefit ladder covered here. VIP is a separate rolling donation-loyalty program with its own tiers and rewards.

What is the difference between Donator Points, Donation Credits and PK Points?

They are separate currencies. Donator Points are used in the Donator shop, Donation Credits are tradeable store-credit items, and PK Points are the general-economy currency.

How are official Roat Pkz ranks or donations claimed, and can the system change?

Buy rank tickets separately through the official store and claim them using its current in-game instructions; total spend alone does not grant a higher rank. Official pages differ between ::reward and ::rewards, so verify the checkout command. Commands, values, areas and requirements can change.
