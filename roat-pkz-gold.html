PK Points

PKP is the currency quoted for normal Roat PKZ gold orders. Include both the quantity and the PKP unit when contacting a6d9.

Donation Credits

Donation Credits are priced separately and should not be assumed to use the same availability or rate as PKP.

Activity-Based Points

Vote, minigame, skilling and similar point balances belong to their own systems and are not included in a standard PKP order.

HOW PKP ENTERS OUR STOCK

How Do We Source Roat PKZ Gold?

Our available PKP comes from Roat PKZ players who decide to exchange currency they no longer plan to use.

Some sellers have accumulated PKP through PvP, trading or long-term activity on the server. Once a sell offer has been reviewed and completed, that currency can later be offered to another player.

The available quantity changes as sell offers arrive and buyer orders are completed. This is why the quoted rate can move with both supply and demand.

A Player-to-Player Supply Cycle

We act as an independent middle point between players selling excess PKP and players looking to purchase it. We do not generate currency or control the Roat PKZ economy.

What does 1M PKP mean?

1M means one million PK Points. A 50M request therefore means fifty million PKP.

Are PK Points and Donation Credits the same?

No. Standard orders on this page refer to PKP. Donation Credits are a separate currency with their own rate and availability.

Can the quoted rate change for a larger order?

Yes. The requested quantity can change the unit rate, so review both the rate and complete-order total before payment.

How do I sell PKP to a6d9?

Send the PKP quantity and requested rate to a6d9. The offer is compared with current buyer demand and stock before it is accepted or declined.

Will a buyer need to provide a Roat PKZ login?

Normally, no. Buying is handled without sharing your Roat PKZ login. Temporary account access may only be discussed with a seller for an agreed direct transfer.

What information may be requested during a direct sell transfer?
