Why players buy gold

Why Do Players Buy Impact Gold?

For many players, buying gold means spending less time rebuilding their bank and more time on the parts of the game they enjoy, such as bossing, PKing or working toward other goals.

Upgrading Their PvM Gear

Better gear can make bossing smoother and help players take on harder PvM content. Some buyers use Impact gold for weapons, armour and supplies rather than spending hours grinding repetitive money-making methods to afford the gear they want or need.

Building a Strong PK Setup

PKing requires weapons, armour, supplies and replacement gear. Some players buy Impact gold to afford better gear and more powerful weapons, experiment with different setups or return to PvP faster after losing a set.

Gambling for Fun at ::gamble

For some players, ::gamble is one of the most entertaining parts of Impact. A bigger bank gives them more gold to use during gambling sessions, but every wager is unpredictable and buying gold never guarantees a win or profit.
