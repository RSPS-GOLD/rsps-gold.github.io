Player-driven supply

Where Does the Impact Gold Come From?

The Impact gold we sell comes from other players. Some get valuable PvM drops, while others build their bank through merching, PvP or a lucky win at ::gamble. When they have more gold than they need, they may decide to sell some of it.

Once a sell order is completed, that gold can be offered to another buyer. Since stock comes from players, the amount available and the rate can change over time.
