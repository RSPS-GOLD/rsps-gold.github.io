IMPACT SKILLING

Impact Thieving Guide

Thieving can give a new Impact account a focused starting activity with simple banking and visible level progression. Use it to establish an early cash stack, then move on when another activity better supports your account.

THE HOME TRAINING AREA

Where Impact Thieving Begins

The official Home Area guide places the Thieving training area just outside Edgeville Bank and says it supports training from the first level through 99. This bank-adjacent location keeps the early loop easy to understand: interact with the available Thieving target, watch your inventory and bank when needed.

Inspect the targets in the live area before starting. Use the NPC or stall your current Thieving level allows, then check the next target’s requirement in game as your level rises. This guide deliberately avoids an old fixed target table because requirements and rewards can change; the live interaction and official information should decide your next step.

Set a short first goal, such as reaching the next available target or filling and banking a few inventories. A short checkpoint lets you test how the skill feels and how it contributes to your bank without assuming it must be your entire progression route.

MOVE WHEN THE ACCOUNT IS READY

Build a Thieving Progression Path

A good path is based on current level access, reliable clicking and convenient banking. Move to a higher target after verifying that you meet its requirement and that the new interaction is actually working. Do not remain on a lower target only because it is familiar, but do not rush upward without checking whether your success rate and attention make the change worthwhile.

Learn the loop

Use the first available target, identify the bank route and watch how quickly inventory space becomes the limiting factor.

Check new access

As levels rise, inspect the next available NPC or stall and verify its requirement in the current game.

Set an exit goal

Choose whether your objective is a skill level, an early cash reserve or enough resources to begin another activity.

Review the path at each target change. If the next option adds complexity but does not help the present goal, there is no need to force it immediately. Thieving is one tool in a wider account plan.

REDUCE LOST TIME

Banking and Inventory Management

The nearby Edgeville bank is the key convenience. Begin with a mostly clear inventory so the reason for each bank trip is obvious. Deposit the output you intend to keep, return to the training area and avoid unrelated bank sorting during the loop.

If rewards or items stack, you may be able to train longer before banking; if they occupy separate slots, inventory space will matter sooner. Confirm the current behavior rather than assuming it. Keep any required item in a consistent inventory slot and protect space for the training output.

Efficiency here is not only actions per minute. A sustainable rhythm with fewer misclicks and clear level checks can be more useful than a frantic session that leads to mistakes. Use natural bank trips to check the skill level and decide whether a new target is available.

EARLY BANK BUILDING

How Thieving Fits Into Money Making

Thieving can help establish early funds while training a skill, but its value depends on the current rewards, your target and market conditions. Do not publish or rely on a fixed hourly rate. Instead, measure a short personal session: record what entered the bank, subtract any costs and compare the result with another activity your account can perform.

Use the proceeds for a defined next step. Early supplies, a basic equipment gap or a small reserve are clearer goals than “becoming rich through Thieving.” Once combat, Slayer, mass bossing or another skill becomes a better match for your preferences, move part of the session there and compare.

KEEP PROGRESSION USEFUL

Common Thieving Mistakes

  • Following an outdated target order without checking live requirements.
  • Training with a cluttered inventory and making unnecessary bank trips.
  • Staying on one target after a more suitable option becomes available.
  • Assuming a fixed money-per-hour result from someone else’s account.
  • Using automation for a repetitive activity.
  • Continuing after the skill no longer supports the current account goal.

PRACTICAL SESSION PLAN

Impact Thieving Checklist

  1. Travel to the Thieving area outside Edgeville Bank.
  2. Confirm the current NPC or stall your level allows.
  3. Clear unnecessary inventory items before starting.
  4. Use bank trips as level and progression checkpoints.
  5. Record the bank result from a short personal session.
  6. Move to a new target or activity when it better supports the goal.
Server-rules note: Repetitive skilling must still follow Impact’s rules. Bots, scripts, macros and other automation are prohibited.
