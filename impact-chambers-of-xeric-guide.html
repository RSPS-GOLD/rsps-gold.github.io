Impact Chambers of Xeric Guide

THE IMPACT COX ROUTE

Entry, Solo and Team Preparation

The official Impact Wiki says to use the teleport tree at ::home and navigate to the Chambers of Xeric entrance. Its CoX page documents Olm, including solo, duo and trio positioning references. That means your first decision is not only equipment; it is the team size and role structure you intend to learn.

A team run lets players divide the mage hand, melee hand and movement responsibilities. A solo run requires one player to manage the full rotation. Start with the format that matches your experience and available teammates. If learning in a team, agree on positions and calls before the pull. If learning solo, expect more attempts to establish consistent movement.

Use the current official guide as the mechanical reference. Impact’s implementation may differ from external Chambers of Xeric guides, and old tile layouts or timing advice may not match the live encounter.

COVER THE HANDS AND HEAD

Gear Styles and Supply Planning

Olm requires different damage styles across the encounter. The official guide describes magic-hand and melee-hand roles, followed by ranged damage to the head in the final phase. Build a setup that covers the role you were assigned and any backup style your team expects.

The official page shows high, middle and budget categories, including Void. Use those as current reference points rather than a requirement to own a specific best item. A learner often gains more from a manageable switch and extra supplies than from carrying every possible upgrade.

Reserve the primary equipment for your hand role, a compact ranged switch for the head phase and any water-based spell or item your current strategy uses for the flame-wall mechanic. Confirm runes, ammunition, charges and special-attack equipment before entry. Keep healing and prayer supplies sufficient for learning, not only for a perfect run.

POSITION BEFORE DAMAGE

Team Roles and Room Communication

The official guide includes distinct positions for trio runners, mage hand and melee hand, plus duo positions. Mark or agree on the relevant locations using the current official reference. Do not reuse markers from another server without verifying them.

The mage-hand player focuses the appropriate style and stays aligned with the team’s turning pattern. The melee-hand player manages that hand and watches for the clench behavior. A runner or coordinated team movement helps control where Olm looks and which attacks affect each player. Exact movement should be learned with the official current examples and practice.

Use short calls for “turn,” “clench,” “both low,” “head,” “acid,” “wall,” “crystals,” “lightning” and “teleport.” Agree on the terms first. During the third phase, call hand health so both are finished close together. If one player is responsible for the flame-wall response, make that responsibility explicit.

Solo preparation uses the same concepts but places every role on one player. Reduce switches and learn one stable movement cycle before pursuing faster methods.

FOUR PHASES, CLEAR PRIORITIES

Verified Olm Phase Structure

Phases 1 and 2: learn the hand cycle

The official Impact guide says either hand can be killed first, while recommending the magic hand to avoid the melee hand clenching and becoming temporarily unavailable. Follow the team’s chosen order and maintain role positioning. Early phases are the best time to stabilize movement because the third phase adds a simultaneous finish requirement.

Phase 3: finish both hands together

In the third phase, both hands need to die at roughly the same time or they return. The official strategy suggests lowering one hand, finishing the other and returning to the weakened hand. Communicate remaining health and stop damage when required. A slightly slower coordinated finish is better than forcing one hand down early and repeating the phase.

Phase 4: ranged head damage

After the hands disappear, Olm’s head becomes the target and the official guide says it must be damaged with Ranged. Equip the prepared switch promptly, but keep watching floor and crystal hazards. Do not spend the start of the phase searching through the inventory.

READ THE VISUAL CUE

Prayers, Spheres and Special Attacks

Olm uses standard magic and ranged attacks. The official guide says either corresponding protection prayer reduces damage. It also documents colored spheres that deactivate prayer and require a matching response: red relates to melee, green to ranged and purple to magic. Learn the colors rather than guessing from the previous attack.

Acid can appear as pools or follow a targeted player. Move without crossing teammates and avoid standing in placed acid. The flame wall traps players between two walls and is handled with a current verified water-based response. Assign that response before the raid so nobody assumes another player will do it.

Crystal mechanics include falling crystals that target a player and bombs that explode around a position. Watch for the documented character or floor cues and move away without pulling hazards through the team. During the head phase, maintain movement awareness even while maximizing ranged damage.

Other documented specials include crystal bursts under players, north-south lightning and a teleport swap. The teleport deals more damage when affected players remain far apart, so move toward the team’s agreed meeting position. For lightning, use the current guide’s timing and route rather than improvising through another player.

BUILD CONSISTENCY

A CoX Learning Plan

First learn the room positions and normal attack prayers. Then practice one full hand cycle without losing the role. Add sphere responses, followed by acid, flame, crystal and teleport mechanics. In Phase 3, make hand-health calls the main priority. In Phase 4, change to Ranged cleanly while continuing to avoid hazards.

Review each failed run by cause. Incorrect prayer and poor movement need practice; hand imbalance needs communication; running out of supplies may reflect avoidable damage or insufficient inventory. Only the last category automatically points toward more resources.

If the team changes, restate roles and meeting positions. A strategy that worked in a trio may need adjustment in a duo. Solo learners should change one element at a time and keep notes on where the cycle breaks.

Raid rewards are variable. A successful run does not guarantee a particular item or profitable result, and stronger gear does not remove the need to execute mechanics.

KEEP THE TEAM SYNCHRONIZED

Common Chambers of Xeric Mistakes

  • Entering without agreed mage-hand, melee-hand and movement roles.
  • Using unverified markers copied from a different version of CoX.
  • Killing one hand too early in Phase 3.
  • Forgetting the ranged switch for the head phase.
  • Dragging acid or crystal hazards through teammates.
  • Failing to meet for the teleport swap.
  • Adding equipment switches before basic movement is reliable.
  • Planning around a guaranteed raid reward.

BEFORE ENTERING COX

Chambers of Xeric Checklist

  1. Select Chambers of Xeric from the ::home teleport tree.
  2. Confirm solo, duo or trio positions from the current official guide.
  3. Assign mage hand, melee hand, runner and mechanic responsibilities.
  4. Check primary equipment, ranged head switch and water response.
  5. Verify runes, ammunition, charges, food and prayer supplies.
  6. Agree on hand-health and teleport meeting calls.
  7. Set a learning budget and treat rewards as variable.
