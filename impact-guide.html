Impact Gear, Gold and Progression Guide

This guide explains how players can plan their gear, gold and progression on Impact. It covers practical PvM and PvP priorities without assuming that every player has the same goals or budget.

THE BASIC UNIT

Understanding Impact Gold

Impact gold is commonly discussed in billions. The abbreviation 1B means one billion coins, so it helps to confirm whether a number is being quoted in coins or billions before comparing offers or planning a purchase.

Players may use their bank for weapons, armour, supplies, utility upgrades and replacement sets. Those goals have very different costs and priorities. A player preparing for a single boss does not need the same bank structure as someone who regularly switches between raids and risky PvP.

Market values and available stock can change as players buy, sell and move between activities. Treat any older value as a reference rather than a promise, and build your plan around the content you actually want to play.

UPGRADE ORDER

Planning Gear Progression

Starting setup

Prioritize a functional setup that lets you complete your chosen content consistently. Leave room for food, potions, ammunition and other recurring costs instead of treating the gear purchase as the entire budget.

Mid-game upgrades

Look for the upgrade that removes the clearest limitation. A stronger main weapon may have more practical impact than a small armour improvement, but survivability or utility can matter more for a different boss or play style.

End-game goals

Large upgrades should fit a clear purpose. Compare the item with the content it unlocks or improves, then keep a reserve so the rest of your setup remains usable after the purchase.

A common mistake is spending the entire bank on one desirable item and then lacking the supplies, supporting equipment or replacement money needed to use it. Let your account goal decide the order: boss progression, raid consistency, PvP variety and collection goals each call for a different path.

REPEATABLE CONTENT

Building a PvM Budget

Start with the encounter rather than a shopping list. Identify the combat style, defensive needs and utility that the content requires, then divide the budget into working parts.

  • Main weapon: the core damage option for the activity.
  • Armour: protection and bonuses that support that weapon.
  • Supplies: consumables for several attempts, not only one run.
  • Utility items: switches or tools that solve a specific mechanic.
  • Reserve cash: room for price changes, repairs or the next sensible upgrade.
  • Replacement costs: a separate allowance where deaths or lost items are possible.

Stronger gear can improve efficiency, but it does not guarantee a drop or a profit. Your familiarity with the encounter, supply use and ability to complete runs consistently still matter.

CONTROL YOUR RISK

Building a PvP Budget

A useful PvP bank covers more than the strongest setup you can equip once. Plan the main loadout, enough supplies for multiple fights and replacement sets for the risk you intend to take. Keep a lower-risk alternative available for practice or rebuilding.

Set the maximum value you are comfortable losing before choosing the items. This makes it easier to separate a routine setup from a higher-risk loadout and prevents one loss from ending your PvP plans. No weapon, armour set or larger balance guarantees a win.

PLAYER ACTIVITY

Ways Players Build Their Bank

Players build Impact banks in different ways. Some focus on PvM drops or events, while others prefer PvP, merching or a lucky win at ::gamble. The mix often changes as an account gains access to harder content or the player finds an activity they enjoy.

Gambling is not reliable income and can reduce a bank as quickly as it increases one. A sustainable plan should work even when a rare drop, event reward or gamble does not go your way.

GOAL-BASED ESTIMATE

How Much Impact Gold Might You Need?

There is no useful fixed recommendation for every player. Instead, write down the goal and sort it into a planning category:

Small upgrade

One focused improvement plus supplies and a modest reserve.

Multiple gear upgrades

A sequence of upgrades that must work together, with each priority listed before you spend.

PvP replacement bank

Your chosen setup multiplied by the number of losses you want to be able to absorb.

Larger end-game goal

A long-term target separated from the money needed to keep playing current content.

Add the items, supplies and reserve together only after you have decided what matters first. This gives you a meaningful total to use when requesting a quote without suggesting that you buy more than your plan requires.

Server-rules note: Server rules can change. Review the current Impact rules before buying, selling or transferring gold.
