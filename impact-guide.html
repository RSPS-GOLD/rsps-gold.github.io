FAQ

Impact guide questions

Which Impact guide should a new player read first?

Start with the Impact Money Making Guide for a bank plan or the Slayer Guide for combat progression. If you are comparing account-benefit tiers, use the Donator Benefits Guide instead.

Which Impact guide covers money making?

The Impact Money Making Guide compares beginner, bossing, raid, PvP and high-level routes. The Gemstone Crab Guide adds a focused low-attention activity with its voting requirement, drops and profit caveat.

Which Impact guide explains Donator ranks?

The Impact Donator Benefits Guide explains cumulative ranks, Spent Impax, helper pets, boss access, private instances, Quick Gear capacity and documented restrictions.

Which Impact guides cover raids, Slayer and skilling?

Use the Theatre of Blood, Tombs of Amascut or Chambers of Xeric guide for the relevant raid. Separate guides cover Slayer, the Thieving route, Hunter and Gemstone Crab mechanics.

How are Impact guide sources and server changes handled?

Each article links to the official Impact Wiki pages, commands or rules used for factual details and separates them from editorial recommendations. Check the linked source and live interface when a decision depends on a value that can change.
