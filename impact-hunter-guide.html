Impact Hunter Guide

TOOLS BEFORE TRAVEL

Starting Hunter on Impact

The official Impact Hunter guide begins with bird snares purchased from Shanomi at ::shop. It directs players to Hunter Island through the teleport tree at ::home. Buy the correct trap type before travelling and carry a spare only if your current plan and inventory allow it.

The number of traps you can use increases with Hunter level. The official guide lists one bird snare before level 20, two from 20 and three from 40 during the early stage. Do not place more than your current level permits. If a trap cannot be set, check the level, location and whether another active trap is already counting toward the limit.

Keep the first session short. Learn how to place, check and reset a trap without losing track of it. Hunter rewards attention to the active area; placing every allowed trap is useful only when you can maintain them.

VERIFIED TRAINING ROUTE

Birds, Chinchompas and the Wilderness Option

The official guide uses birds on Hunter Island from levels 1 to 53. It lists crimson swifts, golden warblers, copper longtails and tropical wagtails at different parts of the island, with each creature requiring a different Hunter level. Follow the live level requirement and move to the appropriate location only after unlocking it.

At level 53, the guide changes to box traps and regular chinchompas on the southern side of Hunter Island. Grey chinchompas begin that stage, followed by red chinchompas at level 63. The number of usable box traps also increases as levels rise, so check the current guide instead of assuming the maximum immediately.

From level 73, the official route offers black chinchompas through ::chins in the Wilderness. This is a risk decision, not simply the next automatic step. The guide recommends bringing a teleport option, but you should also check current Wilderness mechanics, carry only what you accept losing and remain alert to other players. The official route says regular chinchompas can be continued instead if you want to avoid the Wilderness stage.

Bird snares

Train on Hunter Island with the bird your current level allows. Expand the active trap count only at the verified thresholds.

Box traps

Change tools at the verified chinchompa stage. Confirm that you bought box traps before leaving the shop.

Risk choice

Choose between continued island training and black chinchompas only after evaluating current Wilderness risk.

KEEP THE LOOP CLEAN

Inventory and Banking

Carry the trap type for the current creature and keep enough open space to handle the training output. Do not bring both trap types after you have committed to one stage unless you are deliberately testing a transition. A clean inventory makes missing equipment obvious.

Before a longer trip, test what happens to caught creatures and trap items in the current game. Bank when inventory space or accumulated value makes it sensible, not on an arbitrary schedule. For Wilderness training, bank more conservatively and avoid carrying unrelated valuables.

Count your traps when leaving an area. A forgotten trap can interrupt the next session, while carrying too many consumes inventory without increasing the active limit. Keep a small bank reserve of the correct tools so a lost or misplaced trap does not stop progression.

ATTENTION OVER RUSHING

Improve Hunter Efficiency

Arrange traps in a pattern you can scan consistently. Reset completed or failed traps promptly, but avoid rapid movement that causes misclicks. As the allowed trap count rises, add one trap and stabilize the rhythm before using another.

Change creature when the next verified level is reached and the travel or reset pattern still feels manageable. Higher-level access does not help if you spend most of the session searching for traps or correcting movement. Track personal progress by level gained over a short session rather than publishing a fixed XP-per-hour claim.

Hunter can support broader skilling progression and account goals, but reward values and uses can change. Check current in-game information before assigning a fixed value to the session output.

AVOID INTERRUPTED SESSIONS

Common Hunter Mistakes

  • Travelling with the wrong trap type for the selected creature.
  • Trying to place more traps than the current level permits.
  • Moving to a new creature before verifying its requirement.
  • Entering the Wilderness with equipment you did not intend to risk.
  • Using a crowded inventory and losing track of trap items.
  • Forcing a higher trap count before learning a stable reset pattern.

BEFORE EACH STAGE

Impact Hunter Checklist

  1. Confirm the current creature, level requirement and location.
  2. Buy bird snares or box traps as required.
  3. Check how many traps your current level permits.
  4. Clear inventory space and bank unrelated valuables.
  5. Use the teleport tree for Hunter Island, or assess risk before ::chins.
  6. Change stage only when the next route is unlocked and practical.
Server-rules note: Hunter must be trained manually within Impact’s current rules. Automation is prohibited, and Wilderness locations carry player-versus-player risk.
